# Recourse and adjudication-twin evaluation

## Provenance

- Generated by `python -m bench.recourse_eval` on 2026-10-11; deterministic (language model disabled, rule planner and rule fact binder only).
- Policies: the hand-verified sandbox records in `clauseguard/sandbox/persona_policies.py` (tier POLICY_DOC_VERIFIED); four are modelled on real public wordings (Sompo/Roojai HCP, Astra HCP rider, Astra Flexi Life, Chubb MY PA), the rest are mock wordings written for the sandbox.
- Part A replays the 33 scripted tasks in `data/scripted_tasks/` (90 turns) through `agent.loop.handle_turn`; every DENIED verdict is passed to `recourse.find_recourse` (breadth-first, at most 2 legitimate actions, Pareto-minimal by (days, money, effort)) with the persona's whole portfolio.
- Part B reads `data/real/fos_decisions.jsonl` when present and `bench/fos_mapping.json` (manual mapping).
- Raw results: `evidence/recourse_eval.json`.

## Limits

- The FOS mapping is manual: a human reads each decision, picks the closest sandbox policy and writes the facts; the twin is then judged against that reading, not against the decision text itself. A different reader could map a decision differently.
- Sample size: Part A has 6 denials from 33 scripted conversations over three personas and eleven policies; Part B has 3 mapped decisions. Neither supports a rate with a useful confidence interval; the numbers describe this corpus only.
- UK decisions concern UK products and law; the sandbox policies are Indonesian and Malaysian wordings. Only the clause *type* (waiting period, pre-existing, late notification, exclusion) carries over, not the amounts, periods or the regulator's reasonableness test.
- Recourse costs are ordinal (money and effort 0..3) from a small table, not quotes; the time component is exact calendar arithmetic from the policy's dates.
- The twin lists grounds by isolating each clause under the frozen solver; a ground the solver cannot express (for example a reasonableness or disclosure argument) is not listed.
